The app lets you:

  • scan QR codes and barcodes with the camera of your device, or read them from a picture you choose in your photo library;

Code recognition and generation happen on your device. Live scanning needs camera permission; reading a selected photo does not. Scanning can fail or return an incorrect result because of hardware limitations, damage to a code, poor printing or lighting.
