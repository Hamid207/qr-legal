2. Overview

Scanning and QR generation happen on your device. I do not operate a backend that receives your camera images, scan history or saved QR designs. You do not need an app account.

The app has no ads, advertising SDK or tracking across other companies' apps or websites. It does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA). It has no separate general-purpose usage analytics SDK; RevenueCat processes subscription information and provides subscription analytics, as explained in section 4.

Information can leave your device for subscription checks and purchases, when you contact support, or when you choose to share content or open a destination. Device backups are described below.

3. Information on your device

  • Camera. With your permission, the app processes camera frames locally to recognise QR codes and barcodes. It does not record or upload the camera feed. Without camera permission you can still scan a selected photo or create codes. Change camera access in iOS Settings → Apps → QR Code Generator - Main → Camera.
  • Selected photos. The system photo picker gives the app access to the images you select; it does not require general access to your photo library. A picture selected for scanning is processed locally and is not saved as an image in scan history.
  • Logos. If you add a picture as a QR logo and save the design, a copy of the logo image is stored locally with that design so it can be reopened and edited.
  • History and designs. Scan history contains decoded code content. Saved designs contain the encoded content, design settings and any logo. Content can include links, contact information or Wi-Fi credentials. The app does not send these records to me or RevenueCat.
  • Preferences. Settings such as appearance and the scan-history toggle are stored locally.

Recognised scans are saved locally in History. You can delete entries in History; Settings also offers a separate confirmation to remove existing scans. Created designs are separate from scan history and can be deleted in History.

Local records remain until you delete them or delete the app and its data. Exporting or sharing a code can create copies outside the app; deleting its history entry does not remove those copies. Temporary files are also created for exports. Your device's backup settings may include app data in a device or iCloud backup. Deleting local data does not necessarily erase earlier backups; backup management is controlled by you and Apple.

4. Subscription services

Apple. The App Store handles payments, billing and subscription transactions under Apple's Privacy Policy. I do not receive your payment-card details or Apple Account credentials.

RevenueCat. RevenueCat, Inc. processes subscription information on my behalf. Its SDK creates a random app user ID and communicates with its servers to check subscription status, validate purchases and restore access. These checks can occur even if you have never purchased PRO.

This processing includes the SDK-generated ID; device, operating-system and app-version information; purchase and receipt records; last-seen information; and the request's IP address, from which country can be derived. The app does not supply a name or email address to RevenueCat.

The information supports subscription access, purchase restoration, fraud prevention and subscription reporting, such as revenue and renewal statistics. Although RevenueCat calls the ID "anonymous", it distinguishes an installation or subscription record; it is not a promise that all related records are legally anonymous.

RevenueCat does not receive your camera frames, selected photos or QR content from the app. Its Privacy Policy and Data Processing Addendum describe its role and safeguards.

Subscription records are held to administer and restore purchases, resolve billing issues and meet applicable record-keeping obligations. Deleting the app does not automatically delete records held by Apple or RevenueCat. Contact me about RevenueCat records as described in section 8; Apple's records are governed by its own retention rules.

5. Support and actions you choose

The app prepares an editable message containing the app version, device model identifier and iOS version. You can review or remove these details before sending. Your email service and the support mailbox service process the correspondence.

I use this information to answer your request, investigate the reported issue and handle related follow-up. Correspondence is retained for those purposes and any applicable legal or dispute-related record-keeping needs. Do not send passwords, full payment-card details or unrelated sensitive data.

Sharing and destinations. Exporting a code or sending it through the iOS share sheet gives the chosen destination the content you select. Opening a scanned link or another action can send information to the website, app or service involved. Those recipients handle it under their own policies.

A detected web link opens when you choose the open action. The app does not verify the safety of scanned destinations.
